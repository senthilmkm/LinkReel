### Reply Message to App Review:

Dear Apple App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing **LinkReel** (Build 5). We appreciate your feedback regarding **Guideline 4.3(a)** and welcome the opportunity to clarify our app’s unique design, original code architecture, and specialized utility for mobile app creators.

We want to formally assure the review board that **LinkReel is an original, custom-built application and is NOT a template, white-label product, clone, or repackaged app.**

### 1. Proprietary Tech Stack & Custom Codebase
LinkReel was engineered from scratch specifically for indie developers and mobile app marketers. It consists of:
- **Custom React Native (Expo) iOS Client**: Features a dark OLED user interface, interactive storyboard builder, and real-time brand customizer.
- **Dedicated Cloud Backend Pipeline**: Microservices deployed on Google Cloud Platform featuring a Playwright retina scraper engine, structured Gemini 2.0 narrative model, Google Cloud Neural TTS engine, and 4K Remotion video rendering pipeline.

### 2. Specialized Utility & Unique Value Proposition
Unlike generic video creation tools, LinkReel is purpose-built to solve a single, complex problem for mobile app developers: **turning public App Store URLs into 9:16 high-converting promo video reels.**

Key unique interactive features in Build 5 include:
- **App Store URL Extractor**: Safely ingests public App Store metadata, App Icon, and retina screenshots to auto-map visual scenes.
- **4-Scene Storyboard Studio**: An interactive editor allowing creators to fine-tune Scene Hooks, Pain Points, Solution screens, and CTAs.
- **Brand & Visual Customizer**: Full creative control over Brand Accent Colors, Aspect Ratios (9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, 16:9 landscape), Watermark overlays, and CTA badges (*"Download on App Store"*).
- **Neural Voice & Kinetic Subtitles**: Synchronized word-level timecode alignment for studio-quality kinetic captions.
- **Local Asset Integration**: Seamless option for developers to attach custom device screenshots per scene directly from iOS Photos.

### 3. App Store Metadata & Presentation Overhaul
In addition to the binary enhancements in Build 5, we have completely updated our App Store Connect metadata, title, subtitle, and screenshots to clearly communicate LinkReel’s specific role as a developer utility.

We respectfully request a re-evaluation of Build 5. If there are any specific additional features or clarifications you would like to see, please let us know and we will be happy to accommodate immediately.

Thank you for your time and dedication to keeping the App Store safe and high quality.

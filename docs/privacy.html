LinkReel Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy explains what LinkReel collects and why. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Account token on device. A random user ID stored on your iPhone so your free credits and purchases stay on this install. We do not ask for your name or email to use the app.
  • Job data. The App Store URL or story you submit, scene choices, and the generated video so we can make the reel.
  • Purchases. Apple handles payment. We store a product ID and Apple transaction ID so we can add the correct credits and avoid double-granting.
  • Technical logs. Basic error logs to fix failed renders.

What we do not collect

We do not require an account, do not show ads, and do not sell lists of users. We do not use your listing or videos to train public models for others.

How long we keep files

Generated videos and temporary uploads in our storage are deleted after about 7 days. Save to Photos for a copy you keep. Credit balances stay until you delete the app data or ask us to delete the account token.

Third parties

Apple (App Store, In-App Purchase, Photos if you save), Google Cloud (API, storage, text-to-speech, and the model that drafts scenes), and Apple’s public App Store listing data when you paste a link.

Your choices

You can refuse Photos access and still use Share. You can stop using the app at any time. To request deletion of your server records, open an issue on github.com/senthilmkm/LinkReel and include your user ID from the credits screen if shown.

Children

LinkReel is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

The app

What LinkReel does

Plan is free We read the listing and lock 4 scenes. That does not use a credit.
Generate = 1 credit A finished reel with Nora or Alex, captions, and music if you chose it.
3 free reels New installs get 3 generates on this iPhone. Then a one-time pack.
Save to Photos The in-app video link expires in about 7 days. Keep a copy in Photos.

How a reel is made

  • Paste a public App Store URL (apps.apple.com). Play Store links are off for now.
  • Or write the story yourself and attach matching screenshots.
  • Check the 4 scenes. You can edit the on-screen caption and the voice line before you spend a credit.
  • Change or skip a listing screenshot if one is wrong. Skip uses a caption card.
  • Pick size (9:16, 1:1, or 16:9), voice (Nora or Alex), caption style, and music.
  • Generate. A typical render is about 30 seconds, 1080×1920 for Reels.

Common questions

The App Store link did not work
Use a public listing URL, like https://apps.apple.com/app/id…. Private, region-locked, or removed apps cannot be read. Play Store is turned off.
The words on the reel are wrong
On the 4-scene review screen, tap On screen and Voice and fix them before Generate. Planning is free. Generate still costs 1 credit.
Generate failed
Check your connection and try again. If a credit was taken and no video appeared, email senthil930@gmail.com with the time you tapped Generate. We will look up the job.
I bought a pack and still have 0 credits
Open the credits / paywall screen and tap Restore Purchases. If the balance is still wrong after a few minutes, email us with the Apple purchase receipt.
Refunds
Payments are charged by Apple. Request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com. Used credits are not refunded by LinkReel except as Apple requires.
The video disappeared in the app
Hosted files are removed after about 7 days. Save to Photos if you want to keep the reel.
How do I delete my data?
Open credits (⚡) at the top of the home screen. At the bottom of that paywall, tap User ID to copy it. Email senthil930@gmail.com and ask us to delete server records for that ID. Uninstalling the app removes the on-device ID.

What to include in your email

  • What you were doing (plan, generate, buy, restore, save)
  • The App Store link or product name, if relevant
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Approximate time of the problem (your local time)
  • For purchases: the last 4 digits of the Apple receipt or the order date

Do not send your Apple ID password or full card numbers. We never ask for those.
